Who We Are

Our work combines enterprise technology architecture, responsible AI, workflow automation and human oversight. Through consulting, we help organisations adopt Microsoft technologies thoughtfully. Through AskBharatNow, we are building structured clinical workflows designed to support patients, clinicians and healthcare organisations.

We combine strategic advisory with hands-on architecture and implementation. Every engagement begins by understanding the business problem, evaluating whether automation is appropriate and defining a maintainable solution.

We focus on Microsoft technologies because depth of expertise matters more than breadth of tools. When an organisation runs on Microsoft 365, Azure and Power Platform, the AI and automation strategy should extend that foundation rather than fragment it.

Our Commitment to Responsible AI

AI is a powerful tool, but it requires thoughtful implementation—especially in industries handling sensitive data.

Human-in-the-Loop Design

AI assists, humans decide. We design solutions that augment staff capabilities, not replace human judgment in critical decisions.

Security by Design

Controls such as encryption, role-based access and audit logging are evaluated and implemented according to the engagement’s architecture, licensing, data classification and organisational requirements.

Compliance Awareness

Our approach considers privacy, security, governance, auditability and human oversight from discovery through implementation.

Transparency & Auditability

Where technically and operationally appropriate, AI-assisted workflows are designed to support traceability, review and human accountability.

Ethical Implementation

We respect professional ethical obligations—whether that’s attorney-client privilege, patient confidentiality, or CPA independence standards.

Client Education

We explain how AI works, what its limitations are, and how to use it responsibly in day-to-day operations.

Final regulatory compliance depends on the customer’s policies, legal assessment, operational procedures, technology environment and implementation controls.
