About ContextWorks Digital

Senior-led Microsoft AI & automation consulting for organizations that value security and compliance

Our Story

ContextWorks Digital was founded to address a critical gap in the market: mid-size professional service organizations need AI and automation solutions, but they can't afford to experiment with their data security or compliance posture.

We've spent over 15 years working within the Microsoft ecosystem—from on-premises SharePoint and Exchange to modern Azure cloud architectures. We've seen the evolution of AI from experimental tools to production-ready services, and we understand the difference between what's technically possible and what's operationally responsible for regulated industries.

Our focus is deliberate: we work exclusively with Microsoft technologies because depth of expertise matters more than breadth of tools. When your business operates on Microsoft 365, your automation and AI strategy should leverage that existing investment, not introduce complexity with disconnected platforms.

Our Commitment to Responsible AI

AI is a powerful tool, but it requires thoughtful implementation—especially in industries handling sensitive data. Our approach to AI adoption is grounded in these principles:

👤

Human-in-the-Loop Design

AI assists, humans decide. We design solutions that augment staff capabilities, not replace human judgment in critical decisions.

🔒

Security by Design

Data encryption, role-based access, and audit logging are standard in every solution we build, not optional add-ons.

📋

Compliance Awareness

We understand HIPAA, GDPR, and industry-specific regulations. Our solutions are designed with compliance considerations from day one.

🔍

Transparency & Auditability

Every AI interaction is logged. Every automated decision can be traced. You maintain full visibility and control.

⚖️

Ethical Implementation

We respect professional ethical obligations—whether that's attorney-client privilege, patient confidentiality, or CPA independence standards.

📚

Client Education

We explain how AI works, what its limitations are, and how to use it responsibly. No black boxes.
