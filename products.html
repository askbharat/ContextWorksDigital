Problem

Healthcare information remains fragmented

Patients often receive medical information across reports, prescriptions, discharge summaries, and WhatsApp conversations. Doctors and clinic teams spend time reviewing documents, explaining findings repeatedly, and coordinating follow-ups without a unified longitudinal view.

Fragmented Records

Medical information is scattered across hospitals, laboratories, clinics, and personal folders.

Limited Continuity

Previous findings and follow-up notes may not be readily available during the next consultation.

Manual Clinical Workflows

Teams spend time extracting information, reviewing documents, and communicating guidance manually.

Patient Vault

A longitudinal Patient Vault for better continuity

The Patient Vault is designed to organise fragmented health records into a longitudinal patient history. Instead of treating every report as an isolated document, it connects relevant findings across time and supports a better understanding of previous results, treatments, changes, and follow-up requirements.

It aims to support continuity of care through structured organisation, clinician review, and clearer follow-up planning.

Designed to support

  • Structured longitudinal history
  • Changes and trends across reports
  • Continuity across consultations and follow-ups
