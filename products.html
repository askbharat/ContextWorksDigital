Turn fragmented medical reports into structured clinical information, clinician-reviewed patient guidance, and a longitudinal Patient Vault.

AskBharatNow is an AI-assisted, clinician-governed healthcare workflow platform. AskBharatNow helps clinics, diagnostic centres, doctors, patients, and caregivers organise fragmented medical reports, prescriptions, and discharge summaries into usable health information. AI assists with document processing and clinical organisation, while qualified clinicians remain responsible for reviewing and approving patient guidance.

Problem

Healthcare information remains fragmented

Patients often receive medical information across paper files, PDFs, prescriptions, laboratory reports, discharge summaries, calls, and WhatsApp conversations. Doctors and clinic teams spend significant time reviewing these documents, explaining findings repeatedly, and coordinating follow-ups without a unified longitudinal view.

🧾

Fragmented Records

Medical information is spread across laboratories, hospitals, clinics, and personal channels.

🔁

Limited Continuity

Historical findings and prior guidance may not be available when the next consultation begins.

⏱

Manual Clinical Workflows

Teams spend time reading documents repeatedly and coordinating follow-up communication manually.
