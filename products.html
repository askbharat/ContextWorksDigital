AskBharatNow / AI Clinical OS

AI-assisted, clinician-governed healthcare workflows for structured medical records, patient-safe guidance, and continuity of care.

Problem

Healthcare information remains fragmented

Patients often receive medical information across paper files, PDFs, prescriptions, laboratory reports, discharge summaries, phone calls, and WhatsApp conversations. Doctors and clinic teams spend significant time reviewing these documents, explaining findings repeatedly, and coordinating follow-ups without a unified longitudinal view.

Fragmented Records

Medical information is scattered across hospitals, laboratories, clinics, devices, and personal folders.

Limited Continuity

Previous findings, treatments, and follow-up recommendations may not be readily available during the next consultation.

Manual Clinical Workflows

Healthcare teams spend time extracting information, reviewing documents, communicating guidance, and tracking follow-ups manually.

Patient Vault

A longitudinal Patient Vault for better continuity

The Patient Vault is designed to organise fragmented health records into a longitudinal patient history. Instead of treating every report as an isolated document, the platform connects relevant findings across time and helps healthcare professionals understand previous results, treatments, changes, and follow-up requirements.

It is intended to support continuity of care through structured organisation, clinician review, and clearer follow-up planning.

Designed to support

  • Structured longitudinal summaries
  • Clarity across multiple reports and consultations
  • More consistent follow-up planning
  • Patient-safe guidance with human review

Trust and safety

Built around human oversight
