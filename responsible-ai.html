Responsible AI

Effective date: 31 July 2026

ContextWorks Digital follows a responsible AI approach for consulting engagements and AskBharatNow product development.

  • Privacy and consent-aware data practices.

  • Transparency on system capabilities and limitations.
  • Traceability and auditability for critical actions.
