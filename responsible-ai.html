Responsible AI

Effective date: 31 July 2026

ContextWorks Digital follows a responsible AI approach for consulting engagements and AskBharatNow product development.

Core Principles

  • Human oversight in high-impact workflows.
  • Privacy and consent-aware data practices.
  • Security-by-design and role-based access controls.
  • Transparency on system capabilities and limitations.
  • Traceability and auditability for critical actions.

Healthcare Context

AskBharatNow is designed to support workflow efficiency and structured information handling. It does not replace licensed medical professionals.

Continuous Improvement

Responsible AI controls are reviewed as product capabilities evolve and as legal/regulatory expectations change.
